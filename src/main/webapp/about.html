BusGo is India's next-generation online bus ticketing platform, built to eliminate travel uncertainty and deliver safe, dependable, and instant bus connectivity across the country. By offering comprehensive bus schedules across 500+ cities, transparent fares with zero hidden surcharges, dedicated front row passenger safety protection, and live highway GPS checkpoints, BusGo has earned the trust of daily intercity commuters. Headquartered in Madhya Pradesh, BusGo operates active express corridors across Indore, Dharampuri, Bhopal, Ujjain, Pune, and Mumbai.

Management Team

Kirtan Bhawsar, CEO Full Stack Developer

Kirtan Bhawsar has been Chief Executive Officer and Lead Full Stack Developer of BusGo since 2026. Prior to founding BusGo, he specialized in scalable backend architectures, high-concurrency ticket reservation engines, and conversational AI travel assistance. Born and raised in Moti Nagar Colony, Dharampuri (Dhar District, Madhya Pradesh), Kirtan completed his Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree from the Jawaharlal Institute of Technology (JIT), Vidhya Vihar, Borawan. At BusGo, Kirtan spearheads strategic product direction, core database engineering, cloud payment infrastructures, and fleet network operations. Under his leadership, BusGo has pioneered specialized passenger safety standards including dedicated solo female seat reservations and direct 24x7 driver desk lines.

Bhavesh Savner, CTO Frontend Developer

Bhavesh Savner serves as Chief Technology Officer and Lead Frontend Developer at BusGo since 2026. A native of Maha Laxmi Nagar, Motipura Main Road, Khargone (Madhya Pradesh), Bhavesh plays an integral role in setting the company's technology direction, user interface design, and client-side architecture. At BusGo, he leads effective delivery of intuitive interactive seat layouts, live Leaflet highway GPS transit tracking, and high-performing mobile-first web experiences. A frontend specialist passionate about smooth user journeys, Bhavesh strongly believes that clean code and rigorous attention to usability create the ultimate booking experience. He oversees multi-theme consistency and rapid performance across all desktop and smartphone devices.
